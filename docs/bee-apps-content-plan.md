# Bee Apps Content Plan

Blog + comparison content to promote all 6 Bee Apps, published on capaxe.com using the existing blog and `/shopify/compare` infrastructure. 36 blog posts (6 per category) + 18 comparison pages.

## Ground rules

- Every post promoting a Bee app carries a one-line disclosure: "Bee Apps is built by our team at Capaxe Labs."
- Promotion pattern per post: solve the reader's problem first, introduce the app as the automated way mid-post, end with an App Store CTA. Never lead with the pitch.
- Each post links to: the app's App Store listing, 1-2 related comparison pages, and 1 related capaxe service or pattern page.
- Comparisons stay honest — concede what competitors do better; recommend the Bee app for the segment it genuinely wins (usually price + simplicity).
- No trailing slashes in links. Add posts to the existing blog collection with proper categories and tags.

## Cadence

2 posts per week ≈ 4.5 months for all 36. Follow phase order below (matches search opportunity). Roundup/comparison posts publish last in each category so they can link to the how-to posts.

**Positioning note (researched Aug 2026):** llms.txt has ~10% domain adoption but near-zero crawler pickup — a few hundred requests across hundreds of millions of AI bot visits; OpenAI/Google/Anthropic don't list it in crawler docs for search surfaces. Do NOT market Bee AI SEO primarily as an llms.txt generator. Lead with the AEO audit, AI bot accessibility testing, and JSON-LD injection — those affect real citation outcomes. Publishing llms.txt is a cheap hedge, framed as such. This honesty is a differentiator: competitors overclaim, and merchants who test the claims will trust the app that didn't.
